New MSP client onboarding checklist

Miss something during onboarding and you'll be chasing it during an outage.

  1. Business context: headcount, locations, remote staff, hours, compliance needs.
  2. Admin access: email tenant, firewall, registrar/DNS, backup console, vendor portals, stored in your password manager.
  3. Device inventory: tag, model, serial, user, OS, warranty.
  4. Network details: ISP, public IP, firewall, Wi-Fi, subnets, DNS.
  5. Licenses and renewals.
  6. Security baseline: MFA, password manager, backup tests, written policies. Gaps become your first projects.
  7. An agreed SLA.
  8. A maintenance rhythm: monthly checklist and quarterly business review.
