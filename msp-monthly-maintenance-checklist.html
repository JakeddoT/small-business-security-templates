MSP monthly maintenance checklist for small business clients

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Each month, for every client, check six areas: patching, backups, security alerts, accounts and access, licenses and renewals, and hardware health. Then send the client a one-page summary. A repeatable monthly checklist is what turns "we fix things when they break" into managed IT, and the monthly summary is the easiest proof of value you'll ever send.

This checklist is written for small MSPs and solo IT consultants with clients of 5–50 users. Most items take minutes with an RMM; the point is doing all of them, every month, and writing down what you found.

The monthly checklist

1. Patching

  • Windows and macOS updates installed on all devices; list any device more than 30 days behind and why.
  • Third-party apps updated (browsers, PDF readers, Java, Zoom, line-of-business apps where possible).
  • Firewall, router, switch and Wi-Fi firmware checked against vendor advisories.
  • Servers patched in the agreed maintenance window, with a reboot confirmed.
  • Any end-of-life systems still in use listed, with the plan (see Windows 10 end of support).

2. Backups

  • All backup jobs succeeded, or failures were investigated and fixed.
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace backup covering mail, OneDrive/Drive and SharePoint/shared drives.
  • At least one copy offline or immutable, with separate credentials and MFA on the backup console.
  • A spot restore of one file or mailbox item, logged. Do a fuller restore test quarterly (checklist).

3. Security alerts

  • Antivirus/EDR alerts reviewed and closed; every device reporting in.
  • Risky sign-ins, impossible-travel and MFA-fatigue alerts reviewed in Microsoft Entra or Google Workspace.
  • New inbox forwarding rules to external addresses reviewed (a common sign of mailbox takeover; see cleanup steps).
  • Firewall alerts and any new inbound rules or port forwards reviewed. No RDP exposed (check).

4. Accounts and access

  • New and departed staff since last month: onboarding and offboarding completed (offboarding checklist).
  • MFA registration report: every active user registered; exceptions list reviewed.
  • Admin role holders unchanged, or changes approved.
  • Stale accounts (no sign-in for 60–90 days) disabled or confirmed.

5. Licenses and renewals

  • Paid licenses on disabled or departed accounts removed.
  • Domains, SSL certificates, warranties and software renewals due in the next 60 days flagged.
  • Cyber insurance renewal date noted; start the renewal checklist 60 days out.

6. Hardware and health

  • Disk space, disk health (SMART) and battery warnings on laptops.
  • BitLocker still on and not suspended on every laptop (how to check).
  • Devices out of warranty or older than 5 years listed for replacement planning.
  • UPS self-test and battery age for servers and network gear.

Keep it to one page. Owners read color-coded status and one line of notes; they don't read RMM exports.

Tips that make it stick

  • Same week every month. Put it on the calendar for each client.
  • Log exceptions, not just passes. "1 device 40 days behind, reason X" is what protects you later.
  • Roll it into the QBR. Three monthly summaries become the first half of the quarterly business review.
  • Tie it to the SLA. List the monthly checklist as an included service in the managed IT SLA.

Frequently asked questions

What should an MSP check every month?

Patching, backups (including a spot restore), security alerts and risky sign-ins, account changes and MFA registration, licenses and renewals, and hardware health such as disk space and encryption status.

Should MSPs send clients a monthly report?

Yes. A one-page, color-coded summary of patching, backups, security, accounts, renewals and hardware shows the value of the service and documents exceptions.

How often should an MSP test backup restores?

A quick spot restore monthly and a fuller restore test at least quarterly is a common baseline, logged with the date and result.
