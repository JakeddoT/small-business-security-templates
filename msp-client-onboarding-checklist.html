MSP client onboarding checklist

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Miss something during onboarding and you will be chasing it during an outage. Collect these in the first week.

  1. Business context: headcount, locations, remote staff, hours, compliance needs.
  2. Admin access: email tenant, firewall, registrar and DNS, backup console, vendor portals, stored in your password manager.
  3. Device inventory: asset tag, model, serial, user, OS, warranty.
  4. Network details: ISP and account, public IP, firewall, Wi-Fi, subnets, DNS.
  5. Licenses and renewals.
  6. Security baseline: MFA, password manager, backup tests, written policies. Gaps become your first projects.
  7. An agreed SLA.
  8. A maintenance rhythm: monthly checklist and quarterly business review.
