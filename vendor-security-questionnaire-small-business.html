Vendor security questionnaire: 15 questions to ask

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Many breaches start at a vendor. Before a new provider gets access to your systems or customer data, ask a short set of questions and keep the answers on file.

Data and access

  1. What of our data will you access or store?
  2. Where is it stored, and in which country or region?
  3. Is it encrypted in transit and at rest?
  4. Will access be limited to what you need?
  5. Will you use named accounts with MFA to access our systems?

Security practices

  1. Do you have SOC 2, ISO 27001, or a similar independent assessment?
  2. Do you have a written incident response plan?
  3. How quickly will you notify us of a breach affecting our data?
  4. Do you train your staff on security?
  5. Do you use subcontractors who will touch our data?

Contract and continuity

  1. Who owns the data, and how is it returned or deleted at the end?
  2. What are your uptime and support commitments?
  3. How do you back up our data?
  4. Do you carry cyber insurance?
  5. Who is our security contact?

Rate the risk

Mark each vendor low, medium, or high based on the data they touch and the answers. Review high-risk vendors yearly.

Frequently asked questions

Why do small businesses need vendor security reviews?

Vendors with access to your systems or data can be a path for attackers, and insurers and clients may ask how you manage that risk.

What is a SOC 2 report?

An independent audit report on a service provider's security controls, commonly requested from SaaS and IT vendors.
