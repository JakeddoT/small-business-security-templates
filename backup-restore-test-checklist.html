Backup restore test checklist

By Jake Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

A backup you have never restored is a hope, not a plan. Testing takes under an hour and is exactly what insurers want to see.

How to run a restore test

  1. Pick a representative set: a folder of recent work files, a mailbox, or a full system image.
  2. Restore to an isolated location, never over live data.
  3. Open several files and confirm they are intact and current.
  4. Record how long the restore took.
  5. Fix any problems and retest.

Questions your test should answer

  • Is at least one copy offline or immutable, out of ransomware's reach?
  • How old is the newest good copy?
  • Could we restore a whole computer, not just files?
  • Who knows how to run a restore if your usual IT person is unavailable?

What to log

Date, system tested, restore time, pass or fail, issues, and tester. Test at least quarterly.

Frequently asked questions

How often should backups be tested?

At least quarterly, and after any change to your backup system.

What is an immutable backup?

A backup copy that cannot be changed or deleted for a set period, which protects it from ransomware.
