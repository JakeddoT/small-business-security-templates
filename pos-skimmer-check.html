How to check a POS card reader for skimmers

A short inspection at the start of every shift is one of the most effective defenses a small store or restaurant has.

  1. Know what normal looks like. Keep photos of each reader and PIN pad at the register.
  2. Check the card slot and tap area. Anything loose, bulky, glued, or off-color. Overlays often move when tugged.
  3. Check the PIN pad. Spongy or raised keys, tiny holes or cameras above the keypad.
  4. Check cables and seals. New cables, extra devices, broken tamper seals.
  5. Match serial numbers against your inventory. Swapped devices are a known attack.
  6. Verify visitors. Confirm any 'technician' with your manager or POS provider first.
  7. If something is wrong, stop using the terminal, note what you saw, call your manager and processor.

Log each check with date and initials.
