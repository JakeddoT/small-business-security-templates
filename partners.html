Frequently asked questions

Can I share these resources with my clients for free?

Yes. The self-assessment, PDF and guides are free to share, link to, print and include in newsletters, with no sign-up or permission needed.

Do clients have to give an email address to use the assessment?

No. It runs in the browser, needs no sign-up, and doesn't send or store answers.

Is the self-assessment a compliance certification?

No. It checks common baseline controls and does not certify compliance with HIPAA, PCI DSS, the FTC Safeguards Rule or any insurance policy.
