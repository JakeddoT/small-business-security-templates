Tabletop exercise for small business: a 60-minute ransomware scenario

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

A tabletop exercise is a guided discussion where your team walks through a pretend incident. It costs nothing, takes an hour, and reveals gaps in your incident response plan before a real attacker does. Insurers and auditors increasingly ask whether you have done one.

Who should attend

  • The owner or general manager
  • Whoever handles IT (internal or your IT provider)
  • Office manager or operations lead
  • Someone from finance or billing

The scenario (read aloud in stages)

  1. 9:05 AM: A staff member says files on the shared drive have strange extensions and won't open. What do you do in the first 10 minutes?
  2. 9:20 AM: Two more computers show a ransom note demanding payment within 48 hours. Who do you call? Is the network disconnected?
  3. 10:00 AM: Your IT provider confirms ransomware. Backups are your only recovery path. When were they last tested? Were they connected to the network?
  4. Noon: Customers are calling because you can't take orders. What do you tell them? Who speaks for the business?
  5. Next day: The attackers say they copied customer data. Who decides whether notification is required? Do you have counsel and your cyber insurer's number?

Discussion questions

  • Did everyone know who the incident lead was?
  • Did we know how to reach our IT provider, insurer, and counsel after hours?
  • Could we operate manually for a day?
  • Would our backups actually survive this?
  • What is the one thing we will fix this month?

After-action template

Record: date, attendees, what went well, gaps found, actions with owners and due dates. Keep it as evidence and repeat yearly.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tabletop exercise in cybersecurity?

A discussion-based session where a team talks through how they would respond to a simulated incident, to find gaps in plans and roles.

How often should a small business run a tabletop exercise?

At least once a year, and after major changes to staff, systems, or your incident response plan.

How long should a tabletop exercise take?

For a small business, 45 to 90 minutes is usually enough.
