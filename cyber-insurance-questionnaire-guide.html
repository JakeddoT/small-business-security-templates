How to answer a cyber insurance questionnaire

By Jake Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Cyber insurance applications have become stricter, and incorrect answers can affect coverage or a claim. Answer honestly, keep evidence, and fix gaps before renewal.

Common questions and what they mean

  • Do you use MFA for remote access? Every route in from outside: VPN, remote desktop, cloud admin consoles, and email.
  • Do you use MFA for email? All users, not just admins.
  • Are backups offline or immutable? At least one copy ransomware cannot reach or encrypt.
  • Have you tested restoring backups? Keep a dated restore test log.
  • Do you use EDR? Endpoint detection and response on all endpoints.
  • Do you have an incident response plan? A written, reviewed document.
  • How many records do you hold? Count customer, employee, and payment records.
  • Do you use end-of-life software? Anything no longer receiving security patches.

Keep an evidence folder

Screenshots of MFA settings, backup reports, restore logs, training records, and signed policies make renewals faster and support claims.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I answer a cyber insurance question incorrectly?

Misstatements can lead to denied claims or voided coverage. Answer accurately and talk to your broker about gaps.

What controls do cyber insurers require most often?

MFA, EDR, offline or immutable backups, patching, phishing training, and an incident response plan.
