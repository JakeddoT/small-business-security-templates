MSP quarterly business review (QBR) template and agenda

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

A good QBR for a small business client takes 45 minutes and covers five things: what happened last quarter, where their security stands, the top risks, a 90-day roadmap, and budget for the next 12 months. It's the meeting that keeps clients from seeing IT as a cost, and the one where most project work and renewals get approved.

For a 10–50 user client, the owner or office manager wants plain language, a few numbers and clear decisions, not RMM dashboards. Here's an agenda and a one-page template that works.

45-minute agenda

TimeSectionWhat to show
5 minBusiness updateAsk first: new staff, locations, apps, plans, insurance renewal coming up?
10 minService resultsTickets by category, SLA response times met, anything that went wrong and what changed
10 minSecurity postureMFA coverage, backup and restore tests, patch status, EDR coverage, phishing training, open gaps
10 minRisks and roadmapTop 3 risks, recommended projects for the next 90 days, with cost and effort
5 minBudget and lifecycleHardware due for replacement, renewals, licenses, 12-month estimate
5 minDecisions and next stepsWhat's approved, owners, dates

  • Tickets: 46 (down from 58). Top categories: printing 11, email 9, new user setup 6.
  • SLA: 100% of P1/P2 responses within target; 94% of P3.
  • Notable: one blocked account-takeover attempt (MFA stopped it); one failed backup job fixed within 24 hours.

Tips

  • Send the page 2 days before so decisions can happen in the meeting.
  • Lead with their business, not your tickets.
  • Always bring a recommendation and a price. "Should we?" stalls; "We recommend X for $Y" gets decided.
  • Use the monthly summaries. Three monthly maintenance summaries give you most of the scorecard.

Frequently asked questions

What should be in an MSP quarterly business review?

A business update from the client, service results against the SLA, a security scorecard, the top risks, a 90-day roadmap with costs, and a 12-month lifecycle and budget view, ending with decisions and owners.

How long should a QBR be for a small business client?

About 45 minutes is usually enough. Send a one-page summary two days before so the meeting can focus on decisions.

What security metrics should an MSP show in a QBR?

MFA coverage, backup and restore test status, patch compliance, EDR coverage, phishing training completion and incident response plan review, with changes since the last QBR.
