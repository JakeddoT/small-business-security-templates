MFA rollout plan for small business

By Jake Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Rolling out MFA in phases avoids lockouts and helpdesk overload.

Phases

  1. Admin and IT accounts first, using an authenticator app or hardware key.
  2. Email for all users.
  3. VPN and remote access.
  4. Finance and customer-data apps.
  5. Everything else that supports MFA.

Staff announcement template

Subject: Action needed: set up multi-factor authentication by [DATE]. To protect our business and meet insurance requirements, we are enabling MFA on [SYSTEM]. Install [APP] and follow the steps at [LINK] by [DATE]. Contact [IT CONTACT] for help. Never approve a prompt you did not trigger.

Track exceptions

Log any account that cannot use MFA, the reason, the compensating control, who approved it, and a review date.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an MFA rollout take for a small business?

Most small businesses can complete a phased rollout in two to four weeks.

What is MFA fatigue?

An attack where users are spammed with MFA prompts until they approve one. Train staff never to approve prompts they did not start.
