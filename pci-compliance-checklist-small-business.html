PCI compliance checklist for small merchants

By Jake Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

If you accept cards, your payment processor expects you to follow PCI DSS. This checklist helps small merchants get ready. Confirm your Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) type with your processor.

Scope

  • List every system that stores, processes, or transmits card data
  • Use validated P2PE or tokenization where possible to shrink scope
  • Never write card numbers on paper, email, or chat

Network

  • Separate the POS network from guest Wi-Fi and office devices
  • Firewall with default-deny inbound
  • Change default router and terminal passwords
  • Remote access to POS uses MFA and named accounts

Devices and people

  • Inventory terminals and readers with serial numbers
  • Weekly tamper inspections, logged
  • Patching and antivirus on POS PCs
  • Unique logins per employee; access removed on the last day
  • Annual security awareness training

Annual

  • Complete the correct SAQ and Attestation of Compliance
  • Quarterly external scans if your SAQ requires them

Frequently asked questions

Do small businesses need to be PCI compliant?

Yes. Any business that accepts card payments is expected to comply with PCI DSS, usually by completing a Self-Assessment Questionnaire.

How can a small store reduce PCI scope?

Use a validated point-to-point encryption or tokenizing payment solution and segment the payment network.
