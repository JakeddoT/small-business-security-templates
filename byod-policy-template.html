BYOD and remote work policy template

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

When staff work from home or use personal phones and laptops, company data goes with them. A short BYOD and remote work policy sets minimum standards.

Remote work requirements

  • Use the company VPN or approved secure access for internal systems
  • Home Wi-Fi uses WPA2 or WPA3 with a non-default password
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi without the VPN
  • Company devices are not shared with family members

Personal device (BYOD) requirements

  • Screen lock and device encryption enabled
  • Supported, updated operating system
  • Security software where applicable
  • Enrollment in mobile device management or managed apps if required
  • Company files stored only in approved cloud storage
  • Lost or stolen devices reported immediately; company data may be remotely wiped
  • Company data removed at separation

Frequently asked questions

Can a company wipe an employee's personal phone?

Policies typically allow wiping company data or managed apps only. Selective wipe is preferred over full device wipe; state this clearly in the policy.

Is a VPN required for remote work?

For access to internal systems it is strongly recommended; for cloud apps with MFA it may not be necessary.
