Employee offboarding security checklist

The step most often missed: resetting the shared passwords the departing employee knew.

  • Disable email and directory account; revoke SSO/VPN sessions
  • Remove MFA devices and registered phones
  • Reset every shared credential they knew
  • Remove SaaS and password-manager vault access
  • Recover laptop, phone, badges, keys
  • Transfer file and calendar ownership
  • Remove company data from personal devices
  • Review the last 30 days of account activity
  • Archive the account per retention policy
