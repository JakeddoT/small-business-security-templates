Employee offboarding security checklist

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

The offboarding step small businesses miss most often is resetting the shared passwords the departing employee knew: Wi-Fi keys, shared admin logins, vendor portals, alarm codes.

On or before the last day

  • Disable or suspend email and directory accounts
  • Revoke VPN, remote access, and active SSO sessions
  • Reset shared passwords the employee knew
  • Remove access to password manager shared vaults
  • Remove MFA devices and registered phones
  • Revoke SaaS access: CRM, accounting, cloud storage, chat
  • Recover laptop, phone, badges, keys, and tokens
  • Preserve data, then wipe or reimage returned devices
  • Forward or delegate the mailbox per manager instruction
  • Transfer ownership of files, calendars, and shared documents
  • Remove company data from personal devices and confirm in writing

After

  • Review account activity for the final 30 days
  • Archive or delete accounts per your retention policy
  • Log completion in HR or IT records

Frequently asked questions

When should an employee's access be removed?

By the end of their last working day, or immediately for involuntary terminations.

What is most often missed when offboarding employees?

Shared passwords, active sessions on phones and browsers, and SaaS apps outside the main directory.
