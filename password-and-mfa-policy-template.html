Password and MFA policy template

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

Password rules have changed. Current guidance favors long passphrases, password managers, and MFA over frequent forced changes and complex character rules.

Recommended requirements

  • Minimum 14 characters; passphrases encouraged
  • A unique password for every system, stored in a company password manager
  • MFA required on email, VPN and remote access, admin accounts, finance systems, and anything holding customer data
  • Authenticator apps or hardware keys preferred over SMS
  • Change a password immediately if compromise is suspected; no routine forced rotation
  • Separate admin accounts from daily-use accounts, reviewed every 90 days
  • Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start; report it

Frequently asked questions

Should employees be forced to change passwords every 90 days?

Current guidance from NIST recommends against routine forced rotation; change passwords when compromise is suspected instead.

Is SMS acceptable for MFA?

SMS is better than nothing, but authenticator apps or hardware keys are more resistant to phishing and SIM swapping.
