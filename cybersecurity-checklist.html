Small business cybersecurity checklist: 10 controls insurers ask about

Sooner or later a cyber insurer, a big client, or your payment processor will ask how you protect your systems. These are the controls that come up most.

  1. MFA on email. Use an authenticator app rather than SMS where possible.
  2. MFA on remote access and admin accounts. Every way in from outside, and every admin login.
  3. Endpoint protection everywhere. Current antivirus or EDR that someone actually checks.
  4. Backups you can restore. One copy offline or immutable, restore tested yearly.
  5. Patching on a schedule. Within 30 days, sooner for critical. Remove unsupported software.
  6. Phishing training. Report suspicious messages; never approve an MFA prompt you didn't start.
  7. A written incident response plan. Contacts, containment, evidence preservation.
  8. Least privilege. Access matches the job. Review admins quarterly.
  9. Clean offboarding. Disable accounts, revoke sessions, reset shared passwords, recover devices.
  10. Vendor access control. Know which vendors can reach your data; require MFA and named accounts.
