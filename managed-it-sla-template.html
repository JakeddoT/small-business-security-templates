Managed IT SLA template: priorities and response times

By William Todd, IT support and cybersecurity engineer · Updated 2026-11-21

A clear SLA prevents arguments about what counts as urgent. Here is a common structure for small business managed IT agreements.

Example priority levels

  • P1 Critical: business-wide outage or security incident. Response 30 minutes, target resolution 4 hours.
  • P2 High: major function or team impaired. Response 2 hours, target 1 business day.
  • P3 Normal: single user, workaround exists. Response 1 business day, target 3 business days.
  • P4 Low: request or question. Response 2 business days, target 5 business days.

Sections to include

  • Support hours and after-hours terms
  • How to request support
  • Included and excluded services
  • Client responsibilities
  • Fees, term, and cancellation notice

Have any contract reviewed by an attorney before use.
